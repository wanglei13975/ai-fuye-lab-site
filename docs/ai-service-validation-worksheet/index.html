Test one AI service idea before you build a business around it.

Use these seven prompts to check a real customer problem, make a small sample, verify rights and costs, and decide what to do next. This worksheet offers a method, not an income forecast.

KEEP THE TEST SMALLOne customer.
One sample.
One decision.

Do not use private client material or publish AI output until you have the necessary rights and permission.

DAY 01

Name one customer

Who has the problem? Describe one person or small team and the situation in which it happens.

Evidence / notes

What have they already tried? What did you observe or hear directly?

DAY 02

Check the real need

What outcome would make this task easier? Separate a repeated behavior from a polite opinion.

Evidence / notes

What is still uncertain? What small question could clarify it?

DAY 03

Set the limits

List the exact sample, delivery time, tools, direct cost, and revision limit.

Evidence / notes

Can you verify permission for every image, voice, text, and brand asset? If not, do not use it.

DAY 04

Make one small sample

Use your own or authorized material. Make only enough work to show the proposed result.

Evidence / notes

What did the sample take in time and cost? What would you change before sharing it?

DAY 05

Ask with permission

Ask one suitable person if they are willing to review the sample. Make it easy to decline.

Evidence / notes

What did they actually understand, use, or question? Do not treat praise as a purchase.

DAY 06

Check delivery fit

Write the handoff, turnaround, included revisions, and any rights or platform limits.

Evidence / notes

After costs and time, is this small service still practical for you to deliver?

DAY 07

Choose a next step

Continue, narrow the offer, or stop. Base the choice on observed need, sample usefulness, rights, and delivery effort.

Evidence / notes

What is the strongest evidence? What would change your mind? No response is not demand.

Decision

Continue · narrow · stop

Circle one. Write the evidence that led you there and the next action you can complete without making an unsupported promise.

Privacy and safety: this is a static page. Nothing you write on a printed copy is sent to us. Get permission before contacting anyone or using their materials; avoid sharing personal or confidential information with AI services unless you have the right to do so.
