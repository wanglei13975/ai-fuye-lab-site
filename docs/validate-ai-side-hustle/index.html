The seven-day route

Small evidence beats a big guess.

Use the free start to choose a project. Pro adds the complete action routes and copyable client, quote, delivery, and stop-condition templates.

01

Choose one customer

Name one person or small team, one recurring problem, and one outcome they already care about.

02

Make a small sample

Set a narrow brief, record rights and inputs, and estimate the real time and tool cost before promising delivery.

03

Ask for evidence

Use a short conversation or review request to learn whether the sample is useful, understandable, and worth repeating.
